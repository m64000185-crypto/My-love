A letter from me

Happy Birthday ❤️

Happy Birthday to the most special person in my life ❤️ Aaj ka din mere liye sirf tumhara birthday nahi hai, balki woh din hai jise main genuinely celebrate karna chahta hoon, kyunki aaj ke din tum meri life mein aayi thi. Shayad main har baar apni feelings ko perfectly words mein express nahi kar pata, aur kabhi-kabhi meri baatein ya mera behaviour tumhe confuse bhi kar deta hai, but somewhere inside, you really mean a lot to me. Tere saath judi hui har chhoti-badi memory mere liye special hai. Kabhi hum bina kisi reason ke haste hain, kabhi chhoti si baat pe argue karte hain, kabhi ek dusre ko samajhne mein time lagta hai, but at the end of the day, I still value what we have. Mujhe tere saath sirf happy moments nahi, balki woh normal moments bhi pasand hain jahan hum bas apni-apni random baatein share karte hain. I know I have a lot to learn, especially when it comes to understanding things, handling situations and being the person you deserve, but I genuinely want to improve myself. Main ye nahi bolunga ki main kabhi galti nahi karunga, but I can promise that I'll keep trying to understand, learn and become better. Teri happiness mere liye important hai, aur main hamesha chahta hoon ki tu apne dreams ko follow kare, khud par believe kare aur life mein woh sab achieve kare jo tu chahti hai. I hope this new year of your life brings you countless reasons to smile, beautiful memories, peaceful days and people who always make you feel loved and valued. Aur haan, main bas ye chahta hoon ki aane wale time mein hum aur bhi memories banayein — kuch planned, kuch completely random, kuch funny, kuch emotional, but sab apne. Thank you for being a part of my life and for making so many ordinary moments feel special. I may not always say it, but I genuinely appreciate you, I care about you, and I'm really grateful that I get to call you my person. So today, forget everything else for a while, enjoy your day, smile as much as you can and remember that somewhere, someone is genuinely happy that you exist. Happy Birthday once again ❤️ I hope this is just the beginning of another beautiful year of your life, and I hope we get to be a part of many more beautiful moments together. ❤️
